LaTeX Format Enhancer Help

Paste or upload LaTeX into the input pane, select one or more formatters, and press Apply. The formatted result appears in the output pane with changed lines marked green.

  • En-Dash Names converts compound names like Pauli-Fierz to Pauli--Fierz.
  • Explicit Content Mark wraps equation-like environments with percent marker lines.
  • Letter Macros replaces expanded font/accent commands such as \mathbb{R} or \mathcal{A} with preamble shorthands like \bbR or \cA.
  • Equation to Align converts equation and \[...\] display math environments to align form.
  • Explicit Spacing normalizes spacing inside align math content (requires Equation to Align).
  • Labeling adds managed inline labels to sections and supported environments (requires Equation to Align).
