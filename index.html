LaTeX Format Enhancer Help

Paste or upload LaTeX, choose a formatter, and press Apply. Changed result lines are marked with a green background.

  • Labeling adds managed inline labels to sections and supported environments.
  • Equation Content Mark (%) wraps equation-like environments with percent marker lines.
  • Explicit Spacing normalizes spacing inside math content.
  • En-Dash Names converts compound names like Pauli-Fierz to Pauli--Fierz.
  • Equation to Align converts equation and \[...\] display math environments to align or align* form.
